Privacy Policy for Hook & Haul

Effective date: 20 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Hook & Haul ("the Game") handles information when you play the mobile game.

1. Information We Collect

The current version of Hook & Haul does not require you to create an account or provide your name, email address, phone number, or other personal details to play.

The Game stores gameplay progress and settings, such as your current level and sound preferences, locally on your device so that your progress can be remembered.

We do not operate our own servers to collect gameplay information in the current version of the Game.

2. Advertising and Analytics

The current version of Hook & Haul does not display advertisements and does not include advertising or analytics services operated by third parties.

If these features are added in a future version, this policy will be updated as appropriate before that version is released.

3. How Information Is Used

Locally stored gameplay progress and settings are used to provide the Game's features, remember your progress, and retain your preferences between sessions.

4. Data Sharing

The current version of the Game does not transmit locally stored gameplay progress or settings to us or share that information with third parties.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

Gameplay progress and settings remain stored locally on your device until they are overwritten or removed. Uninstalling the Game or clearing its app data may delete locally stored information.

6. Children's Privacy

The Game does not knowingly request personal information directly from children. If the Game's intended audience or features change, appropriate privacy protections and disclosures will be reviewed.

7. Security

Because gameplay progress and settings are stored locally, their protection also depends on the security of your device and its operating system.

8. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the Game's features or data practices change. The updated version will be published on this page with a revised effective date.

9. Contact

For privacy questions about Hook & Haul, contact the developer through the contact details provided on the game's Google Play Store listing.
